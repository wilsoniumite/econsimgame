# Waiting on Stella

Part of the [backlog](../BACKLOG.md). Things only she can settle, gathered on the evening of 11 November 2026 from what helpers and reviewers left for her. None of it holds other work up: each has an answer assumed meanwhile, which is how the game stands today. A letter and number in brackets is a row of one of the checked tables in [sweep-2026-11-11/](sweep-2026-11-11/).

- **The core loop: what helpers found that belongs to it.** Twenty-five rows, nineteen faults, and none is to be taken up before she has said what the loop is. Land beats works, and the landlord comes first on every island (R169). Families own 57 per cent of the works' capital by 1900 (R170). A family that takes every default ends fourth of six, and why has not been traced (R67). The default first trade is usually estates (R172). Industrial rivals are feeble, under a million in 1900 against 17 to 25 million for the landed (R173). AI families never wind down, move their seat or hold, and their choices under the works life cycle were never examined (R175, R124, C103). Hostile buyouts remain round the seat (R176). A player who founds a works every year loses most of them (the founder bot founds 126 to 162 and has 26 to 39 standing, and ends below the idle player on three islands of four), and there is no order to extend a works (R114, C37). A family's works are not rationed by the year's room (R115, C31). Prospects shown as percentages steer the player to tiny works; a hint in money a year would steer better (R171). Every child turning fourteen pauses the game (R174). Under the price rule a family's first purse is worth half as much again (R82, C4). Families cannot buy town ground, and there is five times as much of it round a seat (R110, C115). Families and landlords have no Engel curve (R43). The player's place in the history is nearly always a succession, nine of thirteen kept items on seed 7 (R26, C75). The scripted players' ranks shifted on seed 7, one run each (R20). The politics half is not started, nor research and patents (R165, R166).
