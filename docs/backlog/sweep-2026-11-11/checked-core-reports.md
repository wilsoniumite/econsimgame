# Checked: what the core's builders, reviewers and integrators left open

The 139 items of `gathered-core-reports.md`, each looked at on rust-3d as it stands on the evening of 11 November 2026 (63ab6ca; the simulation's code is as it was at 802b317, the build the last sweep ran), against the log, the rules pages as they now read, and the branches in flight with their briefs in `.claude/night/*.js`. One more was found on a second reading and is at the foot (140). None of this is Stella's note. Nothing in the repository was changed and cargo was not run.

Counts: 91 open, 7 in flight, 22 fixed, 10 moot, 10 not a fault.

How the statuses were decided. **open**: still true on rust-3d and no branch is on it. **in flight**: a named branch's brief or commit covers it. **fixed**: a later commit dealt with it, named. **moot**: the thing it was about is gone or replaced. **not a fault**: the reporter was mistaken, or it is a decision already taken. Where a row is narrower than it was, or is one fault told three times, the last column says so.

What stands out:

- **One fault in three rows, twice.** Output per head that peaks and sags is 36, 100 and 123; the steel rail that does not arrive is 17 and 129; closures in the news 29 and 131; the late railway 89 and 135. Rank each once.
- **The wild year is gone, and not by the cure the reports asked for.** Rows 42, 43 and 62 asked for employment to be rationed. That rule was never built; the causes were mended one by one (prices that go with a district, carters taken on by degrees, a new town's year at its donor's wage), and a month-by-month watch on sixteen seeds finds no town's wage above 2.3 times the middle town's.
- **Kept town ground closed seven rows** (32, 44, 112, 116, 117, 119 in the core, 122) and opened the one that matters most: 128, the real wage and income of 1900.
- **Three rows about saves are moot** (25, 70, 85): a save is a log of orders played again, so no field of the state has to be carried.
- **Rows that read as faults against the port and are not**: grain's swing (125) is the weather the harvests were built to put back; 1750 (136) differs because carters are counted; the later engine works (133) is an accident removed. The farm zigzag (126) is restated: the bar it was held to was set without weather.
- **What waits on Stella**: 11 (exports and the price rule), 53 (which index the player is told), 60 (how much of a harvest is foreseen), 61 (how hard a bad year should bite, now that people answer to it), 73 (toasts for her own works), and with her core loop 37, 75 and 103.
